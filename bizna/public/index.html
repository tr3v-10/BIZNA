    A wallet that divides itself

    How the wallet works

    1. You set the rules

      Name your pots and give each a share, for example Restock 50%, Rent and bills 20%, Savings 15%, Owner pay 15%. Anything you leave unassigned stays as Unallocated.

    2. Money arrives

      A sale payment or a customer repayment is saved, and the amount is divided across the pots to the cent. Unpaid credit does not enter the wallet until the customer pays.

    3. You spend from a pot

      Record an expense against a pot and its balance drops. If a pot runs dry you will see it go negative, so you know exactly where the pressure is.
