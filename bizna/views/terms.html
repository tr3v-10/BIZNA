3. The wallet and pots

The wallet divides the money you record as received into pots using the percentages you set. Moving money between pots, or spending from a pot, changes only the figures inside Bizna. It does not move money in any real account. Percentages apply to money recorded after you save them, not to earlier entries. A pot can show a negative balance if you record more spending than it received; Bizna does not stop you because the spending has already happened.
